## Contract

`concurrency.bounded_map` carries explicit parent job/run identity, execution policy and logging fields into child
threads. A child route is merged back into the parent job’s execution record. Every child closes its own database
connection. It admits at most two local or three API units, with a hard maximum of four, and only applies to job
execution; direct interactive calls remain serial and external batches retain their own executor.

Results return in input order. On the first failure or cancellation, no further unit is admitted; already-running
units finish their documented safe boundary and keep their R4 checkpoint if a paid response completed. Reservations
include pending estimates in budget checks, preventing concurrent calls from each independently passing an unchanged
ledger total.
