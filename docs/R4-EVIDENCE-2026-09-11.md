## Contract

`work_units` stores each completed Findings window or Claims group before the parent artifact is written. Its key
binds the exact request, task, configured model contract, prompt/schema/source/brief/facts revisions, depth and
execution policy. A retry reuses only a compatible completed unit. A source or project revision change may retain
the historical unit but cannot materialize it into the current parent. The parent becomes complete only after every
required compatible unit exists.

Exact duplicate requests serialize through a keyed in-process lock. Unrelated keys remain independent. External
batch results continue to use `batch_items`; R4 does not introduce a parallel batch owner.
