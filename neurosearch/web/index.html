Sources

YouTube video, playlist, channel or search link (results are listed for review) · podcast episode · Instagram reel · direct mp3/mp4 link · web page or article (PDF links too). One per line. Single videos start right away; channels and playlists are listed first, ranked by relevance to your brief (from titles and descriptions — nothing downloaded), and the best ones are pre-selected for you to approve.
Audio/video (mp3, m4a, wav, mp4, mov …) is transcribed with Whisper. PDF, Word, text and markdown are read as documents (citations point at page numbers). SRT/VTT are used as-is. Spreadsheets (xlsx, csv) become searchable and usable as calculators: the chat can change the inputs and read what the workbook's own formulas compute.
Sources already ingested in other projects. Adding one here doesn't re-download anything.
Seen, not added
Everything a channel, playlist, feed or site listing showed you that you didn't pick — kept as cheap metadata (no downloads), so a later gap can find it without searching the web again. Not evidence until added.
Communities
Lived experience, failure modes and practitioner knowledge — never authority. A thread is one source; its posts are the citations. Missions come from what the research is missing (owner regret, unexpected costs, disagreement), not from topics. Exploring lists candidate threads as cheap metadata; you choose what to acquire.
Works
Canonical documents behind your sources — an SBA SOP, an IRS publication, a statute, a book — with their versions and every copy you own or have seen. Copies of one Work count as one line of evidence in Research. Paste an identifier (e.g. SOP 50 10 8, IRS Publication 946, an ISBN or DOI) to resolve it against what you already own.
A small browser extension that runs inside your own logged-in browser. Two jobs: Send this page — any article or guide, including sites that block automated readers (you'll see "blocks automated readers (HTTP 403)" in the queue) or need a login; and Course importer — for courses behind a login (Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific, Skool, Circle, Podia…): it finds every lesson video (Loom, Vimeo, Wistia, YouTube, mp4) and sends them here with your session. No password is ever shared with the app.
  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome / Edge / Brave / Arc open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, pick the unzipped neurosearch-course-importer folder. Pin it to the toolbar.
  3. Click the extension once to set it up: app address …, and the app password.
  4. For an article: open it, click the extension → Send this page. For a course: log in, open the page listing all lessons, click the extension → Scan this course → check the list → Send to Neuro Search.
Videos land below as “Module › Lesson” and are transcribed and analysed like any other source. Session cookies are stored only on this server, in data/cookies/, and are used solely to download those videos.

Project settings

Decisions, constraints & requirements
Recorded automatically when you state them in a chat ("we're going with X", "budget is Y"). Add or remove here.
Spending valve
Estimated API spend across all projects. When a budget is reached the background queue pauses (nothing fails or is lost) and resumes when the window rolls over or you raise the limit. Chat answers and plan builds you start yourself are still allowed.
Health
Can Neuro Search be trusted right now? The database is integrity-checked and snapshotted hourly; every snapshot is reopened and verified before it counts. Every finding's quote is checked against its transcript and every answer's citations against the excerpts it was given.
Danger zone
Deleting the project removes its chats and findings. Sources stay in the library.
